## How to join

The paper is open to scientists working on any aspect of genetic diversity —
across crops, wildlife, microbes, and humans; across measurement, modeling,
conservation, and policy. Joining is a two-step process:

1. **Sign up.** Add your name, affiliation, ORCID, and intended contribution
   to the [author sign-up sheet](/documents/). Anyone in the field is welcome
   to register interest.
2. **Contribute.** Pick a figure, a section, or a Delphi round to weigh in on,
   and complete it within the agreed timeline. Authorship follows substantive
   contribution.

If you would like to discuss a contribution before signing up, contact the
coordinator (see footer).

## Authorship policy

We follow the **CRediT taxonomy** (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) so that each
contribution is acknowledged with specificity rather than collapsed into a
generic "author" role.

To be listed as a co-author, a contributor must:

- **Make a substantive intellectual contribution** to at least one of:
  *conceptualization*, *methodology*, *formal analysis*, *investigation*,
  *data curation*, *writing — original draft*, or *writing — review &amp; editing*.
- **Participate in at least one Delphi consensus round**, including reviewing
  the relevant section(s) and casting votes on contested statements.
- **Approve the final manuscript** before submission.
- **Agree to be accountable** for the integrity of their contribution.

Contributions that are appreciated but do *not* meet the threshold for
authorship (e.g., one-off advice, sharing a dataset without further engagement)
will be acknowledged in the Acknowledgments section.

## Author order

Author order will be:

1. **First &amp; corresponding author** — Moisés Expósito-Alonso (coordinator).
2. **Section / figure leads** listed next, grouped by contribution type.
3. **Consortium contributors** listed alphabetically, with their CRediT roles.

The full author list will be finalized after the last Delphi round and before
submission. Listing order between authors of equivalent contribution will be
decided by the first author with input from the consortium.

## Conflicts of interest

Co-authors must declare any financial or non-financial conflicts of interest
relevant to the paper's recommendations (e.g., advisory roles at seed
companies, conservation NGOs, or government agencies with policy stakes in the
paper's recommendations). Declarations are collected at sign-up and reaffirmed
before submission.

## Diversity &amp; inclusion

We actively seek contributors across career stages, institutions, geographies,
and disciplinary backgrounds. Scientists from regions and communities most
affected by genetic diversity loss are especially encouraged to contribute. If
funding or institutional barriers are keeping you from participating, contact
the coordinator — we will try to help.
