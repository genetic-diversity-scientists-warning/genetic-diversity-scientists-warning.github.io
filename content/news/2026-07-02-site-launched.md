We are launching a small public site for the *Scientists Warning to Humanity
on Genetic Diversity Loss* consensus paper, on July 2, coinciding with the SMBE
Copenhagen plenary.

<figure class="post-figure">
  <img src="/img/plenary-smbe-copenhagen.jpg" alt="Moisés Expósito-Alonso delivering the SMBE Copenhagen plenary, with a slide announcing 'A scientists warning to humanity on the loss of genetic diversity' and a call to vote and sign, plus the site QR code and launch date.">
  <figcaption>Announcing the warning at the SMBE Copenhagen plenary.</figcaption>
</figure>

The site collects:

- **The documents** — manuscript, synopsis, figure list, and supporting Google
  Doc links for contributors.
- **The authorship policy** — how authorship is assigned (CRediT-based), how
  conflicts of interest are handled, and how to join.
- **The Delphi process** — how consensus is being built across the consortium.
- **News &amp; press** — this page, where future announcements will appear.

The collaborative work continues in the shared drive; the site simply makes
the policy and the published parts of the project legible to people outside
the contributor list.

For comment or to coordinate a press release, please contact the coordinator
(see footer).
