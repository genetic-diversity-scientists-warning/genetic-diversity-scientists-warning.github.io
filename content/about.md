## Why a consensus paper

The decline of genetic diversity is documented unevenly across the tree of
life. Crop geneticists, wildlife biologists, microbiologists, and human
geneticists each see fragments of the same erosion, but rarely talk to one
another. A consensus paper does three things that single-lab papers cannot:

1. **Combine evidence across domains** so the global picture is visible.
2. **Identify what most experts agree on** — and where the real disagreements
   are, so future research can target them.
3. **Speak with weight to policy audiences** who otherwise discount single-lab
   findings as one perspective among many.

## The Delphi consensus method

The Delphi method is a structured way to extract consensus (and quantify
disagreement) from a distributed expert panel. It works in rounds:

1. **Round 1.** Each panelist independently answers a set of structured
   questions ("How serious is X? What are the top three drivers of Y?").
2. **Aggregation.** Anonymized responses are tabulated. Areas of agreement and
   disagreement are summarized.
3. **Round 2.** Panelists see the group response and may revise their answers.
   They are asked to justify positions that deviate from the group view.
4. **Iterate** until the answers stabilize.
5. **Final statements.** Items that reach a pre-specified agreement threshold
   become consensus statements in the paper. Items that remain contested are
   reported as open questions.

This avoids two failure modes of large author lists: (a) the loudest voice
sets the position, and (b) every disagreement is papered over with vague
language. Instead, agreement and disagreement are *both* products.

## Timeline

The paper is being built in phases. The current public-facing milestones are:

- **Phase 1 — Scoping.** Outline, figure list, and author sign-up.
- **Phase 2 — Delphi rounds.** Two to three rounds of structured input.
- **Phase 3 — Drafting.** Section leads integrate consensus statements into prose.
- **Phase 4 — Internal review.** Full co-author review and final Delphi pass.
- **Phase 5 — Submission.**

The precise schedule is maintained in the shared
[contributor area](/documents/).
